## Source and calculation

- Source SHA-256: `a56750d701fbac6b49888029368647636766a828bd42948ec4168211bbd5c52f`.
- All four sheets are preserved in `ai-workflow/research/same-tank-500-2026-11-10/source.json` with original headers and values.
- BEFORE HRT and production are read from named columns, retaining source rounding. All 500 rows have the same BEFORE values: 360 h, 66.15 m³/day biogas, 42.09 m³/day methane and 151.07 kWh/day electricity. These are source planning references, not measured plant results or a dynamically learned baseline.
- Features: categorical stage plus AFTER HRT, temperature, pH and SRT. Scenario IDs, before/after output values, percentage gains and verdict labels are not input features.
- A seeded 400/100 stratified holdout fits the scaler and Ridge only on development rows. Final coefficients are then fitted on all 500. Ridge alpha is 10; no output multiplier or gain adjustment is applied.
- 2,000 distinct candidate inputs are generated by convex interpolation between two source rows in the same stage. Predictions are evaluated by the fitted model, not interpolated from output columns. The pool, model, source, request and selected input are fingerprinted.
- Candidate selection stays within the requested stage, requires shorter HRT, and prioritizes >40% predicted gain in all three outputs. A seed selects a different candidate when possible. If no candidate meets that gain target, the best actual model comparison is returned with the unmet goal stated.
- Methane = predicted biogas × predicted methane fraction. Electricity = predicted methane × 9.97 × 0.36. Electricity is gross potential; retrofit energy costs are unavailable. Daily × 365 gives annual projections. Source annual totals remain preserved separately and may differ because of source rounding.

The minimum supplied HRT is 4.2 h, not 2 h. Stage settings expose R1–R6; plain-tank R6 retains its 72.3–119 h coverage and cannot receive a few-hour candidate.

Held-out MAE / R²: biogas 1.48015 / −0.04819; methane 1.92266 / 0.45824; electricity 6.90022 / 0.45832. Biogas does not beat the mean benchmark. These are planning-data scores, not independent plant validation. Prediction intervals are not calibrated. Actual hardware and feed changes are required; improvement is not attributed to software alone.
