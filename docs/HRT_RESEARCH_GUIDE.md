# HRT research workspace

Sign in as Admin. Overview, Biogas, Methane, Electricity, HRT, AI optimizer and reports all use the **hours-scale model**, including AI recommendations. The original day-scale scenario and trained-model tests remain in **Audit Trail** for evidence and plant-validation preparation; they no longer supply the main dashboard.

1. The default **Workbook biogas baseline** mode runs [two separate studies](SEPARATE_OPTIMIZATION_STUDIES_2026-11-06.md): biogas against the supplied 50 m³/day reference, and methane/electricity against the fitted model at 2-hour HRT. Each has its own selected operating point and at least 50% predicted gain; the energy study's biogas decrease is shown explicitly. In **Study settings**, **New HRT scenario on each run** instead generates a comparison HRT within 2–24 hours, differing by at least one hour from the previous comparison, and saves its replay seed. **Enter comparison HRT** keeps a fixed modelled comparison, initially 24 hours. These HRT modes retain their joint-improvement rule; neither converts the historical 22-day baseline into hours.
2. Choose **Shortest HRT**, **Maximum electricity**, or **Specific HRT**. The research model supports 2–24 hours.
3. Set the methane-retention goal. The default 90% means retaining 90% of the highest predicted methane output along the synthetic trajectory. It is not a safety limit.
4. Select **Run AI optimization**. The backend evaluates the fitted model and stores a server-authenticated audit record. Process Monitor also has **Run AI workflow** with an explanatory stage animation; confirmed completed stages come from the backend response.
5. Review **AI recommendations**, HRT, biogas, methane and electricity together. In workbook mode, keep the biogas and energy recommendations separate; their gains do not occur at one operating point. In manual/automatic HRT modes a recommendation must exceed the current modelled biogas and methane outputs by at least one unit in the displayed 365-day totals while meeting the methane-retention goal. Electricity rises with methane under the workbook's conversion assumptions. If the requested target or the 2,000 evaluated HRT points cannot meet those conditions, those HRT modes retain the current prediction without claiming a gain. HRT increases, decreases and unchanged values retain explicit signed labels.
6. Use **AI & KPI reports** to export all 2,000 evaluated points, download the original audit JSON, verify replay, or open the record in **Audit Trail**.

For a manually selected 24-hour comparison and the default shortest-HRT objective with a 90% methane goal, the new joint-improvement rule selects approximately **19.50 hours**. It predicts **69.12 m³/day biogas**, **42.09 m³ CH₄/day** and **151.08 kWh/day theoretical electricity**, compared with approximately **66.15**, **42.09** and **151.07** at 24 hours. These differences are small and are not evidence of a plant-level improvement. A 20.6-hour comparison has no HRT that improves both outputs under this model, so its reported selected values equal its current values. Automatic mode varies the comparison and can therefore return either a modelled joint increase or no improvement.

The same completed run supplies recommendations on every production page and the exported audit record. Editing the settings marks the existing result as stale until a new study finishes. **Ask Copilot** reads the selected saved research record and explains its hours-scale results. It does not use the old day-scale recommendations.

## What the accuracy results mean

The hours model was selected using development folds. A separate 100-row contiguous synthetic block was excluded from fitting and selection, with 20 gap rows separating it from the 380 development rows. On that block, mean absolute errors were approximately **0.408 m³/day for biogas** and **0.587 percentage points for methane content**. The final research artifact was fitted on all 500 rows after evaluation.

Those 500 rows were generated by interpolation and noise from ten source scenarios. They are not 500 independent experiments. Reported scores describe agreement with synthetic data and do not establish plant accuracy. The previously trained day-scale models predict different targets/data, so their scores must not be presented as a direct before/after accuracy comparison with this new model.

The day-model study found no convincing improvement for the SCADA model. A nested assessment of the ten paired reference anchors found a promising alternative, but the small synthetic dataset and differing evaluation protocols do not justify replacing the deployed artifacts. Both original trained model files remain unchanged.

## Data and exports

**Workbook AI reading** shows the original synthetic row IDs, original Excel row numbers and normalized numeric units. The source workbook is unchanged. Fractional methane entries such as 0.64 become 64% for display and model targets.

Research evaluations are generated model points, not additional training data. CSV exports contain their research status, units, source/model identifiers and the original audit record's hash and HMAC. Use the audit JSON for the complete authenticated record. These are server-authenticated exports, not independent public-key signatures.

**Data sources** lists the active and excluded datasets. The extreme 0.5–5.95-hour projection workbook is excluded from this model. HRT input outside 2–24 hours is rejected. No feedstock-specific claim or calibrated prediction interval is provided because the supporting data is absent.

## Preserving the auditor's evidence

The 31 October software corrections remain present: newest-first audit listing and pagination, neutral signed HRT change, server-authentication wording, explicit domain/uncertainty warnings, and the measured-plant validation workflow. Historical records and original model files are not overwritten. Historical day-based inputs retain their actual units, and old research API requests using a days-based comparison remain explicit. No baseline production is fabricated outside the research model's 2–24-hour range. New research records include the comparison-model point and recommendation rows, so the engine fingerprint changes while the fitted model artifact stays unchanged. Old-build records remain exportable; replay requires their matching engine build.

Measured plant validation and demonstrated before/after production improvements remain dependent on equipment arrival, commissioning and appropriate data collection. The research interface cannot establish those outcomes by changing display units or choosing a target.
